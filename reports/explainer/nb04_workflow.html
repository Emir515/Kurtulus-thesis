Master's thesis · MetroAT · NB04

NB04 in five steps

NB04 looks for sensors that misbehave together. It first builds groups of such sensors using only the training months, then checks how those groups behave around the 21 recorded failures.

Part A · training months only

Build the groups

1

Find the unusual windows

The data is cut into 6-minute windows. In each window, every sensor value is compared with its normal range from the training months. A window counts as unusual when at least 5 values are far outside their normal range.

12,022of 64,522 windows are unusual (18.6%). They contain 60% of all failure windows.
In the notebook: z-score per feature against normal training windows; "far outside" means |z| > 3. 258 features after dropping the _sum and context features.
2

Keep only the training months

Only the unusual windows from June 2024 to 11 February 2025 are used to build the groups. The test months stay untouched.

5,351unusual windows from the training months
Why: if the test months helped build the groups, the RQ2 comparison on those months would look better than it really is.
3

Group sensors that misbehave together

For every pair of sensor values, NB04 counts how often they are unusual in the same windows. Values that are usually unusual together end up in the same group.

19groups (FC1 to FC19), built from 126 sensor values
In the notebook: Jaccard distance between features, average-linkage hierarchical clustering, cut at 0.7. Only features unusual in at least 1% of the windows are used.
4

Check that the groups are real

A second grouping method, based on correlation, is run as a comparison, and the tightness of each group is measured.

87%of sensor pairs are grouped the same way by both methods
In the notebook: mean cohesion 0.63 (every group above 0.3); the 0/1 grouping keeps the structure of the real values in 16 of 16 groups.
Part B · whole year

Use the fixed groups

5

Study the groups around the 21 failures

The 19 groups are now fixed. NB04 checks when each group is active in both the training and the test months, and asks four questions.

Does a group warn before a failure?Not days ahead. FC5 and FC7 warn a few hours ahead.
Is a group busier on failure days?Yes in both periods: FC2 and FC6.
Do the values get shakier before a failure?Only FC18, slightly (1.25×).
Is a group just reacting to 2025 changes?9 groups are much busier in 2025 without failures nearby.
Example: FC5 (CW2 compressor switching) is active in the 24 h before 15 of the 21 failures, where about 8 would be expected by chance, about 14 h ahead.

Next: NB05. NB05 scores each group as an alarm and builds the sheet for the Wiener Linien experts, who decide which subsystem each group belongs to.
